# Pathfinder: AI-Driven Life Strategy & Career Architecture

**Pathfinder** is an intelligent life-planning application designed to bridge the gap between long-term human ambition and daily tactical execution. Unlike standard to-do lists, Pathfinder uses AI to act as a strategic mentor, helping users define their "North Star" (Life Goals) and automatically generating detailed, step-by-step execution roadmaps to achieve them.

It features a dedicated decision-making engine ("Ask Pathfinder") that analyzes your unique profile, constraints, and aspirations to provide personalized, data-backed advice for complex life choices.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🗺️ AI-Generated Tactical Roadmaps
Turn vague ideas into concrete plans. When you set a goal (e.g., *"Master Backend Engineering"*), Pathfinder's AI engine:
- Breaks it down into chronological **Milestones**.
- Estimates **Time-to-Completion** for each step.
- Provides **Mentor Tips**, **Key Concepts**, and **Curated Resources**.
- Tracks your progress visually on the Dashboard.

### 2. 🌟 Life Ambitions Architecture
Define your long-term vision (5-10+ year horizon) across key dimensions:
- **Career**
- **Health**
- **Finance**
- **Relationships**
- **Personal Growth**

These serve as the anchor for your tactical goals. The AI uses these ambitions to ensure every short-term action aligns with your long-term purpose.

### 3. 🧠 "Ask Pathfinder" Decision Engine
A sophisticated AI consultant for your dilemmas.
- **Input:** Ask a complex question (e.g., *"Should I switch jobs now or wait?"*) and provide optional scenarios.
- **Context:** The AI reads your *Profile*, *Active Goals*, and *Life Ambitions* before answering.
- **Output:** A structured report containing:
  - **Summary & Recommendation**
  - **Reasoning** (Why this path fits *you*)
  - **Immediate Actions**
  - **Risk Analysis**

### 4. 📊 Intelligent Dashboard
- **Visual Progress Tracking:** Real-time progress bars for all active roadmaps.
- **AI Suggestions Widget:** Proactively suggests new goals or focus areas based on your current profile gaps or stated interests.
- **Life Goal Alignment:** See at a glance how your daily tasks contribute to your life's work.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### Frontend
- **Framework:** React 19 (via Vite)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Modern, dark-mode focused UI)
- **State Management:** React Hooks & Context
- **Routing:** React Router v7

### Backend
- **Runtime:** Node.js & Express
- **Database:** MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
- **Authentication:** JWT & Bcrypt
- **API Architecture:** RESTful

### AI Core
- **Inference:** Hugging Face Inference API
- **Models:** Supports open weights models (e.g., `google/gemma-2-9b-it`)
- **Integration:** OpenAI-compatible client structure for flexible model swapping.

---

## 📂 Project Structure

### Backend (`pathfinder-backend/`)
- `models/`: Mongoose schemas for `User`, `Goal` (Tactical), `LifeGoal` (Ambition), and `DecisionSession`.
- `services/`: Core business logic, including `roadmapGeneratorAI.js` (AI Roadmap generation) and `pathRecommender.js`.
- `routes/`: API endpoints for goals, profile management, and AI analysis.
- `utils/`: Helper functions for parsing AI responses.

### Frontend (`pathfinder-frontend/`)
- `src/pages/`:
  - `Dashboard.jsx`: Central hub for goals and AI suggestions.
  - `AskPathfinder.jsx`: Interface for the decision engine.
  - `LifeGoals.jsx`: Management of long-term ambitions.
  - `CreateGoal.jsx`: Form to initialize new AI roadmaps.
- `src/api/`: Centralized Axios client with interceptors.
